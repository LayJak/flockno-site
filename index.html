Privacy by design

Your home search is not the product.

Most FlockNo state stays on your device. Network requests are limited to the public data sources needed to provide surveillance context and to feedback you explicitly choose to submit. FlockNo has no user accounts, analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
